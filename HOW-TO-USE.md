# How to use UPI Safety Check

Open the app. There is no login: it opens straight on the sample business, and everything below works on it.

## At the counter

**Fraud check** (home). Answer what you know about a payment and press **Check payment**. You get one word (Safe, Check first, Likely fraud), the reasons, and what to do. Below that you can compare the four models.

**Check a payment.** Type the 12-digit bank reference (UTR) and the amount, then **Check**. Green means found and the amount matches. Amber means found but the amount or status is different. Red means not found. Use **Check for fraud** to open the payment in Fraud check, or **Add this payment** if you saw the money in your bank.

**Bank record.** Money in your bank. **Add payment** takes a reference, amount, date and customer. **Import CSV** reads a file with the columns UTR, amount, date and customer. What you add stays in this browser.

## Running the business

**Dashboard.** The top sentence says what you sold, what reached your bank and what needs attention. Use the date buttons, branch and status to narrow it. Click a day, a slice, a branch, a heatmap cell or a risk bar to filter the other charts, and remove the filter with the chip. Click any number card to see those payments.

**Payments.** Five tabs: All, Received, Refunded, Not received, Risky. Search by reference. Click a row for details and a **Check for fraud** button. **Export CSV** saves what you see.

**Missing money.** One chart from sales to money in the bank, and four causes. **See payments** opens the records behind each one.

**Risk alerts.** Counts of Safe, Check and Fraud payments, and a queue of flagged ones with up to three reasons. Press **Fraud** or **Fine** on each. Your answers are saved for the next retrain.

**Action center.** What to do now, this week, and what you marked done.

**My data.** *Add your data*: four boxes (Payments, Orders, Refunds, Settlements), each with its columns, an example row and a template to download. A green tick means the file looks right. Press **Use my data**. *Data explorer*: browse each table, read what each column means, and see charts of your data.

## Help

**Scam messages.** Paste an SMS or WhatsApp message and press **Check**. Below, the three message models are compared.

**How it works.** The flow, how the models were trained and tested, a short glossary and the limits.

## Good to know

- The sample is synthetic. The fraud scores say nothing about real UPI fraud.
- A flag is a reason to look, not proof. Confirm large amounts in your own bank app.
- Uploaded files are kept in memory for 2 hours and then discarded.
- Your recent checks and the payments you add are kept in this browser only.
